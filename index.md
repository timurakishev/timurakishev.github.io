### [about me]
I'm an Asst. Prof. in Linguistics at [KIMEP University](https://www.kimep.kz/) in Almaty, Kazakhstan [08/2023–present]. I'm also an Ole Miss Rebel, holding a Ph.D. in Second Language Studies with an emphasis in Applied Linguistics from the [University of Mississippi](https://olemiss.edu/) [05/2023].

### [interests]
My interests include corpus linguistics📚, generative syntax🌳, phonetics👂, and NLP 💻. I enjoy exploring linguistic data, finding patterns in language, and working with corpora and computational tools. I also teach linguistics courses and collaborate with students on research projects.

### [this website]
I put this website together to share my papers, projects, teaching materials, and occasional thoughts on things I find interesting.
