I'm a linguist and professor at KIMEP University in Almaty, Kazakhstan. My interests include corpus linguistics, syntax, phonetics, and NLP. I enjoy exploring linguistic data, finding patterns in language, and working with corpora and computational tools. I also teach linguistics courses and collaborate with students on research projects.

I put this website together to share my papers, projects, teaching materials, and occasional thoughts on things I find interesting.
