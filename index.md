# About Me

I'm a linguist and professor at KIMEP University in Almaty, Kazakhstan. My main interests include corpus linguistics, syntax, phonetics, and natural language processing.

My research explores how language works, with a particular interest in Kazakh. I enjoy working with linguistic data, exploring patterns in language, and using corpora and computational tools to investigate linguistic questions.

I also teach a variety of linguistics courses and work with students on research projects.

Here, you'll find my publications, research projects, teaching materials, and other things I'm working on.
