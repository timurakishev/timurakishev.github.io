#### [1] Guess the Accent (completed)
**Guess the Accent** is a listening game in which students hear recordings of speakers reading the same English passage and try to identify their accents. The recordings and reading passage come from the [Speech Accent Archive](https://accent.gmu.edu/) at George Mason University, a great resource for exploring accent variation in English.
